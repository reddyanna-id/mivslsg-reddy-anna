The Stadium

 

The Wankhede Stadium has witnessed some of the most memorable moments in the history of cricket - be it Sachin Tendulkar century against England at the 2011 world cup or the six that gave India the ultimate victory in this world cup. Tonight is another chapter to that history. The intimate geography of the place, small forwards, very colorful crowd, sea force blowing in few Marine drives, all these creates a set up that was not present anywhere. The pitch will provide bounce and good carry to the keeper and rewarding good length bouncing as well as injuring anything that is short or over the top. As the dew is likely to settle between the 15th over and onwards, the fast bowlers will find it hard to hold the ball and to so much transform the game itself into a batting game in the second innings.
